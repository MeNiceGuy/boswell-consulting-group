About

Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA, is a business operations and strategy consultant. He has run Boswell Consulting Group since 2015, working with operators on the practical side of running a business: operations, numbers, and planning.

He holds a Doctor of Business Administration in Strategy and Innovation from Capella University, and an MBA (3.97 GPA) and a BSBA from Purdue Global. Alongside the consulting practice, he coordinates veteran-directed care services at Bay Aging.

The resources on this site are built the way he works: plain language, concrete steps, and tools you can actually use.
